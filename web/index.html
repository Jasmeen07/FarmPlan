Most likely issue

Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB)

High Confidence
Symptoms to verify:

Water-soaked lesions on leaf margins turning yellow-orange and drying with wavy edges.

PAU Official Guidance:

Do not apply excess nitrogen fertilizer. Drain standing water and allow field to aerate. Avoid spraying unrecommended antibiotics.

Source: PAU Package of Practices for Kharif Crops (Disease Management p. 48)
